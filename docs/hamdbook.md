1. Welcome to Eclipse Bistro
Welcome to Eclipse Bistro! We are a fictional neighborhood bistro combining seasonal cuisine, warm hospitality, and a relaxed yet refined atmosphere.

Our name represents the moment when different elements come together to create something memorable. The same principle guides our team: every employee contributes to the guest experience.
Our mission
To provide excellent food, thoughtful service, and a welcoming atmosphere where every guest feels valued.
Our core values
- Hospitality: Treat every guest with genuine warmth.
- Quality: Maintain high standards in food, service, and presentation.
- Teamwork: Support colleagues during busy and challenging shifts.
- Integrity: Be honest, responsible, and accountable.
- Cleanliness: Maintain a safe and hygienic environment.
- Respect: Value every employee and guest regardless of background.

3. Professional conduct
All employees are expected to:
- Arrive prepared and ready to work at their scheduled start time.
- Treat colleagues, guests, and suppliers respectfully.
- Avoid arguments in guest-facing areas.
- Never report to work under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Never consume alcohol while on duty.
- Use restaurant equipment responsibly.
- Report mistakes, accidents, and operational concerns promptly.
- Avoid personal phone use while actively serving guests or preparing food.
Personal relationships must not interfere with workplace responsibilities or create conflicts of interest.
4. Attendance and scheduling
Standard scheduling policy
- Employees receive their schedules at least one week in advance whenever possible.
- Shift changes require supervisor approval.
- Employees should arrive 10 minutes before their scheduled shift to prepare.
- Employees must clock in using the restaurant's designated timekeeping system.
- Working off the clock is prohibited.
Calling in sick
If an employee cannot work due to illness, they must notify the supervisor as soon as possible, preferably at least two hours before the shift.

The employee should provide:
1. Their name and scheduled shift.
2. The reason they cannot attend, without disclosing unnecessary medical details.
3. Their expected availability for the next shift.
Example: An employee scheduled for 16:00 who develops a fever should contact the supervisor immediately and must not handle food while ill.
5. Uniform and personal hygiene
Uniform standards
- Clean restaurant-issued shirt or approved uniform.
- Dark trousers or approved workwear.
- Closed-toe, non-slip shoes.
- Hair secured away from the face.
- Minimal jewelry in food preparation areas.
- Clean nails; artificial nails are prohibited for employees handling unpackaged food.
Employees must wash their hands:
- Before starting work.
- Before handling food.
- After using the restroom.
- After handling raw meat, poultry, seafood, or eggs.
- After touching waste, phones, money, or cleaning chemicals.
- After coughing, sneezing, or touching their face.
Handwashing must use soap and running water for at least 20 seconds.
6. Guest service standards
The Eclipse guest experience
Every guest should experience a welcoming, attentive, and professional service.

Service sequence:
1. Welcome guests promptly.
2. Confirm reservations and guide guests to their table.
3. Provide menus and explain specials.
4. Ask about allergies before recommending dishes.
5. Take orders accurately and repeat them back.
6. Deliver food promptly and check guest satisfaction.
7. Offer additional drinks or desserts when appropriate.
8. Present the bill when requested.
9. Thank guests sincerely.
Handling complaints
Employees should use the L.E.A.R.N. approach:
- Listen without interrupting.
- Empathize with the guest.
- Apologize for the experience.
- Resolve the issue within their authority.
- Notify a supervisor when necessary.
Servers may offer a replacement dish or escalate a complaint. Refunds, complimentary meals, or discounts require supervisor approval.
7. Food safety and allergen management
Food safety is everyone's responsibility.
Temperature guidelines for this fictional handbook
Food category	Target requirement
Refrigerated foods	5°C or below
Frozen foods	-18°C or below
Hot holding	63°C or above
Reheating	75°C throughout
Cooling	Follow the approved rapid-cooling procedure

These are illustrative operating targets. Actual procedures must comply with applicable local food safety requirements.
Allergen management
Eclipse Bistro recognizes the following major allergens:
- Gluten-containing cereals
- Crustaceans
- Eggs
- Fish
- Peanuts
- Soybeans
- Milk
- Nuts
- Celery
- Mustard
- Sesame
- Sulphites
- Lupin
- Molluscs
When a guest reports an allergy:
1. Record the allergy clearly on the order.
2. Inform the kitchen directly.
3. Check the official ingredient and allergen information.
4. Prevent cross-contact using clean equipment and surfaces.
5. Never guess whether a dish is safe.
6. Inform the supervisor if the kitchen cannot safely accommodate the request.
Critical rule: Never promise an allergen-free meal unless the kitchen has verified that it can safely prepare it.

11. Workplace safety and emergencies
General safety rules
- Report hazards immediately.
- Keep emergency exits clear.
- Use appropriate protective equipment.
- Follow safe lifting procedures.
- Never operate unfamiliar equipment without training.
- Report injuries, burns, cuts, and near misses.
Emergency procedures
Fire: Raise the alarm, evacuate using the nearest safe exit, and call emergency services.

Serious injury: Contact emergency services, notify the manager, and provide first aid only within your training.

Power failure: Stop unsafe food preparation, keep refrigerator doors closed, and follow the manager's food safety instructions.

Emergency number in Romania: 112.

Retaliation against an employee who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.

16. Disciplinary procedures
Depending on the seriousness of the issue, management may use:
1. Informal coaching.
2. Formal written warning.
3. Final written warning.
4. Further action, up to and including termination, subject to applicable law.
Serious misconduct may require immediate investigation and appropriate action.

Examples of serious misconduct include theft, violence, deliberate food safety violations, harassment, and falsification of records.

Employees will have an opportunity to explain their perspective during investigations.

18. Frequently asked questions
Q: What should I do if I am running late?

A: Contact your supervisor as soon as possible and provide your estimated arrival time.

Q: Can I serve a dish if I am unsure whether it contains an allergen?

A: No. Verify the ingredients and preparation process with the kitchen before serving.

Q: Who can authorize a guest refund?

A: The Restaurant Supervisor or General Manager.

Q: What should I do if a refrigerator temperature is above 5°C?

A: Notify the kitchen supervisor immediately, record the temperature, and follow instructions to assess food safety.

Q: Can I swap shifts with another employee?

A: Only after receiving supervisor approval and ensuring the schedule is updated.

Q: What should I do if I witness harassment?

A: Report it to a supervisor or the General Manager. Retaliation is prohibited.

Q: What is the emergency number?

A: 112 in Romania.

Q: Who is responsible for cleaning the dining tables?

A: Servers are responsible for cleaning and resetting tables after each guest.

Q: Can I promise a guest that a meal is allergen-free?

A: Only when the kitchen has verified that the meal can be prepared safely.

Q: What happens if I make a mistake with a guest's order?

A: Inform the supervisor, apologize to the guest, and follow the restaurant's correction procedure.
